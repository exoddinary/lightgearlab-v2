Good to know.

Do you support projects you didn't build?

Yes. We start with a health check so we understand the system before taking it on, and we'll be honest if a rebuild makes more sense than a repair.

How is the SLA decided?

By how complex and how critical the system is. We agree response times, support hours and what's covered with you before anything is signed.

Can we switch from support to our own team later?

Of course. The handover gives your team the code, accounts and documentation to take over smoothly.

What happens if something breaks outside support hours?

That depends on your plan. Critical systems can include extended hours; for others, monitoring catches issues early and we respond within the agreed times.
